The bigger picture: Class 12 → career

Use this as a flexible guide, not a compulsory sequence. Some routes are professional qualifications or competitive exams rather than degrees.

1. Complete Class 12Choose a stream and subjects based on your interests, strengths, and the eligibility rules of courses you may want.
2. Apply through the relevant routeDepending on the course: CUET-UG, JEE Main/Advanced, NEET-UG, CLAT, NATA, institute tests, auditions, portfolios, merit or other approved routes.
3. Complete a bachelor's degreeMany bachelor's programmes take about 3–4 years; professional and specialised programmes may take longer (for example, architecture or medicine).
After bachelor's, choose your next step: enter employment; pursue a master's degree; complete a professional qualification (such as CA/CS, where relevant); prepare for competitive exams; build a business; or develop a portfolio and freelance.

Entrance exams, subject prerequisites, course duration and recognition vary by institution and year. Check the current official prospectus before applying.

Stage 2: what comes after a master's?

A master's degree is optional for many careers. Choose it when it supports your goals, subject interest or required qualification.

Employment: apply for roles related to your specialisation and build experience.
Research / PhD: explore doctoral study or research roles where eligible; admissions and funding requirements vary.
Further specialisation: pursue regulated professional training, certifications or advanced technical skills where useful.
Entrepreneurship / public service: start a venture, work independently or prepare for suitable competitive examinations.

Master's programmes commonly take 1–2 years depending on the degree and institution; many Indian programmes are 2 years. Verify the current programme duration and eligibility.
