    Stage 2: what comes after a master's?

    A master's is optional for many careers. Choose it when it supports your goals, your subject interest or a required qualification.

    Employment: roles linked to your specialisation.
    Research / PhD: doctoral study where eligible; admissions and funding vary.
    Further specialisation: regulated training, certifications or advanced skills.
    Entrepreneurship / public service: start a venture or prepare for competitive exams.

    Master's programmes commonly take 1–2 years (many Indian ones take 2). Eligibility shown here follows general UGC/AICTE national norms; specific university rules apply and may differ.
